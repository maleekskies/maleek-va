How we'd work together

A straightforward path from first message to an operating rhythm, no long contracts or guesswork about what happens next.

01

You reach out

Send a message over email with a rough picture of your portfolio and what's currently taking up your time. No form to fill in first.

02

A scope conversation

We talk through what you actually need help with, whether that's one recurring task or the full spread of operations and admin work, and agree what's in scope.

03

Onboarding

I get access to what's needed (calendar, records, listing platforms) and set up the systems, folder structures, and trackers the work will run on.

04

Ongoing support

Day-to-day communication runs over WhatsApp and email, with regular summaries so you always know where things stand without having to ask.
