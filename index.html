About Me

I'm Malik, a dedicated Virtual Assistant with hands-on, paid experience supporting UK, US and Canada residential landlords. I don't just understand property management in theory, I've done the work: listed rooms, chased defaulting tenants, booked gas safety checks, coordinated contractors, reconciled rents, and kept everything organised so my clients never have to worry about the details.

Whether you manage long-term residential tenants in the UK or US, run a short-term rental on Airbnb or VRBO, or both, I can take the daily operations off your plate so you can focus on growing your portfolio.

Multiple units

Landlords managing several properties who need consistent admin and rent tracking.

Airbnb & VRBO

One point of contact

Owners who want a single reliable contact for tenants, contractors, and paperwork.
